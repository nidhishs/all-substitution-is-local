\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Active Learning and Batch Redundancy.}
Uncertainty sampling \citep{LewisGale1994} and Query-by-Committee \citep{SeungOS1992} initiated the line of work on selecting queries the learner is least sure about, the latter using disagreement among sampled hypotheses as the uncertainty signal.
\citet{BalcanBeygelzimerLangford2006} introduced the agnostic-PAC active learning framework via the $A^2$ algorithm, and \citet{Hanneke2007} gave general label-complexity bounds for active-versus-passive learning via the disagreement coefficient.
BALD \citep{Houlsby2011} generalises committee disagreement via the mutual information between label and parameters; BatchBALD \citep{Kirsch2019} addresses batch redundancy, since independent BALD picks correlated queries, using a greedy joint-MI criterion to diversify batches.
BADGE \citep{Ash2020} applies $k$-means++ seeding to last-layer gradient embeddings under predicted pseudo-labels, selecting points that combine large gradient magnitude (uncertainty) with gradient-direction diversity.
Loss-driven Bayesian active learning \citep{Huang2026} targets downstream decision loss directly rather than parameter uncertainty.
Each method approximates the rival-action criterion we prove, via sampled posteriors (QBC, BALD, BatchBALD), gradient embeddings (BADGE), or loss-weighted divergences (loss-driven BAL).

\paragraph{Value of Information.}
\citet{RadnerStiglitz1984} showed that single-channel VoI is nonconcave in precision, because at priors where the optimal action is locally constant any small signal carries zero marginal value.
\citet{DeLaraGossner2020} give a necessary-and-sufficient condition, within a general convex-analytic framework, for single-signal VoI to vanish; our bridge lemma is the PWLC specialization, and the cross-channel results of Theorems~\ref{thm:interior} and~\ref{thm:converse} are outside their fixed-agent, single-structure framework.
\citet{ChenWaggoner2017} gave submodularity-based definitions of informational substitutes and complements, a pointwise-at-every-posterior strengthening, and universal characterizations across decision problems; the cross-channel interaction $\Delta\VoI(j \mid i, b)$ studied here is implicit in their marginal-value formulation.
The Bayes-plausibility framework \citep{AumannMaschler1995,KamenicaGentzkow2011} expresses the value of a signal as an expected Jensen gap of the posterior-dependent value function, the form used in Proposition~\ref{prop:decomposition}.

\paragraph{Adjacent Frameworks.}
\citet{GolovinKrause2011} give conditions under which an information-acquisition objective is adaptively submodular, so that greedy policies are near-optimal; our rival-action criterion localizes this global structure to a pointwise test on decision-region membership.
Partial information decompositions \citep{WilliamsBeer2010,Chicharro2017,Kolchinsky2022} split multivariate mutual information into redundancy, synergy, and unique atoms, an information-theoretic question distinct from the sign of $\Delta\VoI$ characterized here.
